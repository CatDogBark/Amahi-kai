# Code of Conduct

Amahi-kai's issues, pull requests and discussions are for making it a better home server.

## Expected

- Be civil, and assume the other person means well. Argue about the idea, not the person.
- Stay on topic: one problem or idea per issue, with what someone needs to understand it.
- Respect the maintainer's time and decisions. Amahi-kai is looked after by one person.

## Not accepted

- Insults, personal attacks, threats or harassment.
- Spam and advertising, including issues and pull requests generated by a tool and posted without
  being read and checked by the person posting them.
- Posting someone else's private information.
- Posting a security problem in public: see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## What happens

The maintainer may edit, hide or delete comments, close issues and pull requests, and block anyone
who doesn't follow this. To report someone's conduct, email catdogbark@pm.me.
